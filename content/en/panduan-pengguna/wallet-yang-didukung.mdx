## Supported Wallets

KORAMP uses a **wallet-first** model: your wallet address is your identity. No account, email, or password required. KORAMP **never** asks for your private key or seed phrase.

## Solana Wallets

For transactions with **SOL** on Solana Mainnet:

| Wallet | Platform | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Phantom** | Browser extension, Mobile | [phantom.app](https://phantom.app) |
| **Solflare** | Browser extension, Mobile | [solflare.com](https://solflare.com) |
| **Backpack** | Browser extension, Mobile | [backpack.app](https://backpack.app) |

Other Solana wallets compatible with the Wallet Standard are also supported.

## EVM Wallets

For transactions with **ETH** (Base Mainnet) and **BNB** (BNB Smart Chain Mainnet):

| Wallet | Platform | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **MetaMask** | Browser extension, Mobile | [metamask.io](https://metamask.io) |
| **Rabby** | Browser extension | [rabby.io](https://rabby.io) |
| **WalletConnect** | Protocol (mobile-friendly) | [walletconnect.com](https://walletconnect.com) |

All wallets supporting WalletConnect v2 are compatible with KORAMP.

## How to Connect Your Wallet

1. Go to [koramp.xyz/topup-sell](https://koramp.xyz/topup-sell).
2. Click the **Connect Wallet** button.
3. Select your ecosystem: **Solana** or **EVM**.
4. Choose your wallet from the list, then approve the connection in your wallet.
5. Your wallet is connected — your address will appear on the page.

<Callout type="info" title="Connection Security">
KORAMP only requests permission to read your wallet address and sign transactions you explicitly approve. KORAMP never requests full custody or your private key.
</Callout>

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use a hardware wallet (Ledger)?**
Yes, hardware wallets connected via MetaMask or Phantom are compatible with KORAMP.

**Can I use an exchange wallet (Binance, OKX)?**
Not recommended. Use a self-custody wallet to ensure you receive crypto directly to a wallet you control.

**Can I change wallets after starting a transaction?**
No — the wallet address connected when the quote is created is the locked destination address. Start a new transaction if you want to use a different wallet.
