## How to Top Up on KORAMP

Top Up lets you buy crypto using Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) via QRIS payment. No account required — just connect your wallet.

## Steps

### 1. Open KORAMP

Go to [koramp.xyz/topup-sell](https://koramp.xyz/topup-sell). Make sure you are in **Top Up** mode (not Sell).

### 2. Connect Your Wallet

Click **Connect Wallet** and choose your wallet:
- **Solana**: Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, or any compatible Solana wallet.
- **EVM (Base / BNB Chain)**: MetaMask, Rabby, or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet.

KORAMP never asks for your private key or seed phrase.

### 3. Select the Asset to Buy

Choose one of the supported assets:
- **SOL** — on Solana Mainnet
- **ETH** — on Base Mainnet
- **BNB** — on BNB Smart Chain Mainnet

### 4. Enter the IDR Amount

Type the amount of Rupiah you want to pay. You'll see an estimate of the crypto you'll receive along with a fee breakdown:
- **Spread** — difference from the market price
- **Service fee** — KORAMP platform fee
- **Network fee** — gas / blockchain transaction cost
- **Tax** — as applicable

### 5. Lock the Quote

Once confirmed, the quote is locked — the price will not change during your session.

<Callout type="info" title="Price locked">
The price shown at quote creation is the final price you pay. KORAMP does not change the price unilaterally after confirmation.
</Callout>

### 6. Pay via QRIS

Scan the QRIS code using any Indonesian banking app or e-wallet that supports QRIS (BCA Mobile, BRImo, Livin' by Mandiri, GoPay, OVO, etc.).

### 7. Receive Crypto in Your Wallet

Once your QRIS payment is confirmed, crypto is automatically sent to your connected wallet address. Delivery typically takes **a few minutes** depending on network conditions.

## Tips & Notes

- Make sure your wallet is connected to the correct network before starting.
- For SOL: if the recipient wallet doesn't yet have an Associated Token Account (ATA), an ATA creation fee is automatically deducted from the SOL output.
- KORAMP operates 24/7 — you can Top Up any time.
