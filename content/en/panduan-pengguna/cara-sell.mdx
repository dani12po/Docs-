## How to Sell on KORAMP

Sell lets you exchange crypto for Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) sent directly to your bank account. No account required — just connect your wallet and enter your bank details.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Page and Switch to Sell Mode

Go to [koramp.xyz/topup-sell](https://koramp.xyz/topup-sell), then click the **swap button** (two-way arrow icon) in the middle of the form to switch to **Sell** mode.

### 2. Connect Your Wallet

Click **Connect Wallet** and choose your wallet:
- **Solana**: Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, or any compatible Solana wallet.
- **EVM**: MetaMask, Rabby, or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet.

### 3. Select the Asset to Sell

Choose the asset you want to sell:
- **SOL** — from Solana Mainnet
- **ETH** — from Base Mainnet
- **BNB** — from BNB Smart Chain Mainnet

### 4. Enter the Amount

Enter the amount of crypto you want to sell, or the IDR amount you want to receive. The system calculates automatically. You'll see a fee breakdown:
- **Spread** — difference from market price
- **Service fee** — KORAMP platform fee
- **Network fee** — deducted from IDR payout
- **Tax** — as applicable

### 5. Enter Your Bank Account Details

Fill in the destination bank account for the IDR payout:
- **Bank name** (BCA, BRI, BNI, Mandiri, and others)
- **Account number**
- **Account holder name** (must match the name on the account)

<Callout type="warning" title="Verify Bank Details">
Double-check your account number and holder name. KORAMP cannot reverse payouts sent to incorrect bank details.
</Callout>

### 6. Lock the Quote

Confirm the transaction details. The quote is locked and you receive a crypto deposit address.

### 7. Send Crypto to the Deposit Address

Send crypto from your wallet to the provided **deposit address**. Make sure:
- The amount sent is **exactly** what is requested.
- You use the **correct network** (Solana for SOL, Base for ETH, BNB Chain for BNB).

<Callout type="info" title="Blockchain Confirmation">
The system waits for confirmation from the blockchain. Confirmation times vary: Solana is typically seconds, Base and BNB Chain a few minutes.
</Callout>

### 8. Receive IDR Payout to Your Bank

Once blockchain confirmation is received, KORAMP automatically sends the IDR payout to your registered bank account via MoonPay. Payouts typically arrive within **minutes to a few hours** depending on banking hours.

## Tips & Notes

- Do not send more or less crypto than the requested amount — this can cause the transaction to fail or be rejected.
- Save the TX hash of your send as proof in case you need to contact support.
