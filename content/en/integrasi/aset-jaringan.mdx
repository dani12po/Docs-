## Supported Assets

| Asset | Network | Chain ID | Ecosystem |
|---|---|---|---|
| SOL | Solana Mainnet | — | Solana |
| ETH | Base Mainnet | 8453 | EVM |
| BNB | BNB Smart Chain | 56 | EVM |

Ramp (Top Up and Sell) is active on all three.

<Callout type="info" title="Mainnet">
KORAMP operates on mainnet with real assets. All transactions use native tokens on production networks.
</Callout>

## Adding EVM Networks to Your Wallet

If your wallet doesn't recognize the network, you can add it manually:

**Base Mainnet (8453)**:
- Chain ID: `0x2105`
- RPC: `https://mainnet.base.org`
- Explorer: `https://basescan.org`
- Currency: ETH

**BNB Smart Chain (56)**:
- Chain ID: `0x38`
- RPC: `https://bsc-dataseed.binance.org`
- Explorer: `https://bscscan.com`
- Currency: BNB

## SOL ATA Fee

For SOL Top Up, if the recipient wallet doesn't yet have an Associated Token Account (ATA), an ATA creation fee (`solAtaFeeIdr` in `FeeConfig`) is deducted directly from the SOL the user receives. This covers the ATA creation cost.
