## Supported Assets

| Asset | Network | Chain ID | Testnet | Wallet type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SOL | Solana | — | Devnet | Solana |
| ETH | Base | 84532 | Sepolia | EVM |
| BNB | BSC | 97 | Testnet | EVM |

Ramp (Top Up and Sell) is active on all three. Mainnet support and additional assets are on the roadmap.

## Adding Testnet Networks to EVM Wallets

If a wallet doesn't recognize the testnet chain (MetaMask error 4902), auto-add it:

**Base Sepolia (84532)**:
- Chain ID: `0x14A34`
- RPC: `https://sepolia.base.org`
- Explorer: `https://sepolia.basescan.org`
- Currency: Sepolia Ether (ETH)

**BSC Testnet (97)**:
- Chain ID: `0x61`
- RPC: `https://data-seed-prebsc-1-s1.binance.org:8545`
- Explorer: `https://testnet.bscscan.com`
- Currency: tBNB (BNB)

## SOL ATA Fee

For SOL Top Up, if the recipient wallet doesn't yet have an Associated Token Account (ATA), an ATA creation fee (`solAtaFeeIdr` in `FeeConfig`) is deducted directly from the SOL the user receives. This covers the ATA creation cost.
