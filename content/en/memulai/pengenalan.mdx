## What is KORAMP

KORAMP is a wallet-based (wallet-first) crypto on-ramp and off-ramp service. Users can exchange Rupiah into crypto (Top Up) and conversely sell crypto back into Rupiah (Sell) without creating an account or registering.

The product targets retail users who want to transact with wallets they already own, as well as developers who want to integrate IDR and crypto ramps into their applications.

## Wallet-First Principle

KORAMP does not store private keys or seed phrases, and has no user table for customers. A wallet address is the identity. This means:

- No customer accounts, emails, or passwords.
- No custody over user funds.
- Transaction history remains traceable through wallet addresses in the public ledger.

## What You Can Do

- **Top Up**: pay with Rupiah via Xendit QRIS, receive crypto to your wallet.
- **Sell**: send crypto to the platform deposit address, receive Rupiah payout to your bank account via Xendit Payout v3.
- **Quote**: locked-in pricing for both Top Up and Sell sides, complete with spread, service fee, network fee, and tax.

## Assets & Networks

KORAMP currently supports three assets on testnet networks:

- **SOL** on Solana Devnet.
- **ETH** on Base Sepolia (chain 84532).
- **BNB** on BSC Testnet (chain 97).

Ramping (Top Up and Sell) is live on all three. Mainnet support and additional assets are on the roadmap.
