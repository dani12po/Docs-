## What is KORAMP

KORAMP is a **wallet-first crypto on/off-ramp marketplace** for Indonesia. Users can exchange Rupiah into crypto (Top Up) and conversely sell crypto back into Rupiah (Sell) without creating an account or registering.

The product targets retail users who want to transact with wallets they already own, as well as developers who want to integrate IDR and crypto ramps into their applications.

<Callout type="info" title="Production Status">
KORAMP operates on **mainnet** with real transactions using real Rupiah. Current stats: **Rp 850M+** IDR volume processed, **12,400+** total transactions, **99.9%** uptime. Software MVP · Not a licensed financial service.
</Callout>

## Wallet-First Principle

KORAMP does not store private keys or seed phrases, and has no user table for customers. A wallet address is the identity. This means:

- No customer accounts, emails, or passwords.
- No custody over user funds.
- Transaction history remains traceable through wallet addresses in the public ledger.

## What You Can Do

- **Top Up**: pay with Rupiah via MoonPay (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or bank transfer), receive crypto to your wallet. MoonPay acts as merchant of record and handles KYC and compliance.
- **Sell**: send crypto to the platform deposit address, receive Rupiah payout to your bank account via MoonPay.
- **Quote**: locked-in pricing for both Top Up and Sell sides, complete with spread, service fee, network fee, and tax.

## Assets & Networks

KORAMP supports three assets on mainnet networks:

- **SOL** on Solana Mainnet.
- **ETH** on Base Mainnet (chain 8453).
- **BNB** on BNB Smart Chain Mainnet (chain 56).

Ramping (Top Up and Sell) is live on all three. Payments are processed by MoonPay (merchant of record) and payouts go to bank accounts.
