## Current Stance

KORAMP applies wallet screening as part of security operations. The current focus is:

- Abuse and fraud detection.
- Rate limiting and progressive IP bans.
- Internal audit trail via `AuditLog` and `LedgerEntry`.

## What It Is Not

KORAMP does **not** claim coverage equivalent to dedicated compliance/analytics providers:

- No third-party wallet-screening service is integrated for sanctions/risk scoring today.
- No Travel Rule data exchange (see [Compliance](/docs/en/privasi-kepatuhan/kepatuhan)).

## Roadmap

Integration of compliance/AML/Travel Rule tooling is a Phase 4 item. See the [Roadmap](/docs/en/roadmap).
